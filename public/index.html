Different Ethiopian banks print references differently. Here is how to locate yours:

Commercial Bank of Ethiopia (CBE) New CBE Mobile Banking creates an 8-character token in the shareable link. Legacy slips start with FT... (e.g., FT240101ABCD) and need the credited account's last digits.
Ethio Telecom Telebirr Look at your Telebirr SMS or transaction history. Reference usually starts with TB or is a 10-14 digit transaction number.
Dashen Bank / Amole Located on the payment receipt SMS or app transaction voucher. Starts with DB or 8-12 numeric characters.
Bank of Abyssinia (BoA Apollo) Found on the Apollo transfer voucher or core banking receipt. Also requires your 5-digit account suffix.
